A window that changes
like the moon does — in phases.

Native desktop apps written in Luau, drawn by Rust and GPUI. Every mod lives through init, update and view — and survives every reload.

Start buildingRead the guide

init

State begins — or is carried over from the last reload.

update

Messages arrive; state moves on.

view

Luau returns a tree of elements, checked by Rust.

draw

GPUI paints it natively, every frame.

“Save the file, and the window redraws in place — keeping each mod's state.”— how Moonframe reloads

A stylesheet

Colors, corners, shadows and animations, by type and class, with themes that reach the terminals.

Providers

Sandboxed WebAssembly or plain processes, for everything Luau can't touch.

Your sky over ours

~/.config/moonframe lays your mods over the app. Updates never touch it.
